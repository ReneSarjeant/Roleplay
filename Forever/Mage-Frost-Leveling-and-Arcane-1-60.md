# WoW Forever Frost Mage Leveling & Arcane Guide

**For René Sarjeant · Solo single-target questing · Levels 1–60**

A single-target Frost plan for solo questing from levels 1–60, followed by a separate level-60 Arcane build. No area-of-effect grinding or gathering packs.

**Beta and endgame note:** Planning guide based on late-October 2026 beta information. Level 60 and endgame performance have not been fully tested, and Blizzard may change the tree. Check the live talent names, rank limits, prerequisites, and unlocks before spending points. The Arcane build is a single-boss, pre-raid simulation—not a proven best build or a solo-leveling test.

## Frost plan: levels 1–9

Normal talent points begin at level 10. Start with Frostbolt from range once learned. Keep Frost Armor and Arcane Intellect active when available; conjure water and drink as needed. Obtain and upgrade a wand to finish low-health enemies without spending mana.

## Frost talent order: levels 10–60

**Winter’s Chill:** Blizzard redesigned it during beta to build toward a stronger Cone of Cold. Cone of Cold affects an area, but can be used against one nearby enemy; no pack pulling is required. If you prefer never to cast it, revisit those five points against the live talent tree.

## Single-target solo routine

1. Start at range with Frostbolt and continue casting as the target approaches.
2. Use Ice Lance when Frostbite freezes the enemy or Fingers of Frost gives a frozen-target opportunity.
3. If the enemy reaches you, use Frost Nova to create space, then reposition. Be mindful of other nearby enemies.
4. Finish a nearly defeated enemy with your wand when it saves mana. It is a regular tool, not merely an emergency button.
5. Use Ice Block in an emergency, keep Ice Barrier available once learned, and drink between fights when needed.

Frost Nova and Cone of Cold can affect nearby enemies: watch your surroundings, especially near neutral creatures or controlled targets. Use Frost Blast as a fast slow when appropriate and available.

## Wands and your first points

René takes Improved Frostbolt at levels 10–14 as agreed. A good wand remains valuable while leveling: use it often to finish enemies and conserve mana. Wand Specialization comes at 41–42 in this plan. Taking it earlier is a valid alternative but delays the Frost milestones; it is not the selected order here.

## Separate Arcane build at level 60

Keep Frost for solo play and try Arcane as a separate specialization when ready; dual specialization becomes available at level 40. The proposed **37 Arcane / 11 Fire / 3 Frost** allocation is a one-minute, single-boss pre-raid simulation. Endgame results and live talent requirements may change. Allocate from the top of each tree downward, respecting tier and prerequisite requirements.

## Learning Arcane

1. Use Arcane Blast as your main building spell.
2. When Missile Barrage activates, use the empowered Arcane Missiles.
3. Use Arcane Wrath for a heavier hit when available.
4. Use Arcane Power for a damage window while watching its increased mana cost.
5. Presence of Mind makes an appropriate cast-time spell instant; the simulated build uses it with Pyroblast.

Arcane Blast and other mechanics changed during beta; consult current tooltips and adjust the exact sequence to your mana and live client. This is a starting point rather than a proven optimal rotation.
