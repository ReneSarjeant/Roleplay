Brinn’s Holy Priest Leveling Guide

Levels 10–60 · Healing from 15 · Reset at 30 · Holy + Shadow from 40

The new plan

Holy is Brinn’s primary role. Prepare for early dungeon healing without paying for an immediate reset. Keep the two points already in Wand Specialization and one in Spirit Tap. Every new point goes toward Holy until the planned reset at 30. Do not finish Spirit Tap in this healing build.

At 30, reset all talents and rebuild as pure Holy: this removes Spirit Tap and the two wand points, bringing Holy milestones forward. At 40, keep Holy and unlock a separate Shadow specialization for solo questing when affordable. Each specialization has its own complete allocation; their points are not shared.

These are recommended leveling builds, not a claim of universal maximum healing. Throughput, mana efficiency and surviving damage all matter. Assumes one normal talent point per level from 10, with no additional Legacy talent points. Talent notation is Discipline / Holy / Shadow.

1. Current build: levels 10–30, before the reset

The first three points are already spent. Begin with the next unspent point. At 15 the allocation is 2/3/1, with Improved Renew 3/3. Baseline healing spells, trained ranks, suitable gear and sensible pull sizes make early dungeon healing possible; deep Holy talents are not required to begin.

4. Around level 40: add Shadow as the second specialization

Dual Specialization becomes available at 40. Buy it when funds permit; if that is later, keep healing and questing as Holy until then. Do not reset the Holy build into Shadow. Allocate the second specialization independently. At exactly 40, its 31 points all go into Shadow, including Shadowform. Wand Specialization waits until 41–42 in this second build.

Healing from level 15 onward

  • Train current healing ranks. Before Flash Heal is learned, use Lesser Heal or Heal as available; do not plan around spells you have not learned yet.
  • Use direct heals for sustained tank damage. Add Renew when its ticks will be useful; avoid clipping it or spreading it across healthy targets. Flash Heal is for urgency once trained.
  • Shield to prevent dangerous damage or protect a cast. Use Fade if enemies turn toward you, and move toward the tank so they can recover them.
  • Keep Divine Grace accessible: the Human priest’s level-10 emergency heal targets an ally below half health and cannot target Brinn herself. Train and use dispels and disease removal when available.
  • Carry water, drink between pulls, and tell the tank when mana is low. Intellect, Spirit and healing gear support the role; keep useful Stamina rather than trading away all survivability.
  • Holy Binding is passive self-healing from Flash Heal on someone else; it is not a separate Binding Heal button. Litany rewards alternating useful healing spells—do not cast an unnecessary spell just to trigger a refund.
  • At 40, use Prayer of Mending for expected incoming damage and switch to Holy before dedicated healing. Shadowform prevents casting healing spells.

Solo questing

Before dual spec, quest as Holy with Smite, Holy Fire when trained, and your wand. Spend mana according to enemy health rather than applying every damage spell. The wand remains usable after the level-30 reset, just without its talent bonus. Heal between pulls and drink as needed.

With Shadow available, use Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast and Mind Flay as appropriate, and Vampiric Embrace for sustain. Do not expect a damage rotation to replace direct healing when someone is in danger. Devouring Contagion can spread to nearby enemies; watch crowd control and extra pulls. Shadow Word: Death can hurt Brinn when it fails to kill; avoid careless use at low health.
