# Aelindra Sarjeant — Character Personality & Roleplay Guide

## Established foundation

Aelindra Ser'jaen'thal is a young Skyborne High Order hunter born and raised on Zephras Isle. She chose observation and the bow over arcane theory, and she is still learning to track on solid ground. A human trainer called her Sarjeant when he struggled with her surname; she kept the useful name. She is observant, practical, quietly wry, curious, and willing to admit when she does not know an answer. Her mother’s pearl earrings connect her to home.

## A suggested personality

Warm, adventurous and quietly playful, with a serious side when companions need her. She might enjoy a gentle joke or a new discovery, then become focused when a trail or a dangerous situation demands it. Her youth can show as impatience or delight; she need not be endlessly cheerful. This interpretation is a suggestion, not a new fixed piece of her history.

## With strangers and friends

With strangers, she may begin politely and observe before offering more of herself. A useful question, shared task, or interest in an animal can open a conversation. With friends, her formality can soften into teasing, laughter, and honest questions. She can be friendly without assuming that anyone trusts her immediately.

## Under pressure

She checks what she can see, communicates plainly, and chooses a useful next step. She can be uncertain, make mistakes, or ask for help. She is a capable island-trained hunter who is still learning mainland tracks; neither her eye for the wind nor Sylthara’s senses are infallible.

## Voice and humor

Her Common is clear and measured, with a touch of elven formality that relaxes in familiar company. She favors concrete observations and honest questions over long speeches. Her humor can be dry, affectionate, or gently self-directed. Let references to the sky come naturally rather than putting wind in every sentence.

## Habits and mannerisms

She often checks the sky before the ground, then takes her time studying a trail. She might glance toward a companion before choosing a path, adjust a feather caught in her gear, or notice her mother’s pearls when reminded of home. These are optional cues for play, not actions required in every scene.

## Sylthara and Thalorien

Sylthara is her Prideclaw cat and regular companion on hunts and in dungeons. Young Thalorien (thah-LOR-ee-en), her Ursera bear, usually joins peaceful gatherings while Sylthara handles the more dangerous outings. Aelindra treats both as animals with needs and limits. Keep their responses open, and ask before another character feeds or touches them. Only the companion present needs to appear in a scene.

## Home and what she knows

Zephras and the sky are home. Her parents are High Order scholars of modest standing; their deaths and present whereabouts are not established. Aelindra did not personally live through the ancient Highborne exile. She may choose what she shares about her family, name, and island. Preserve her existing story and do not assign new victories, losses, or relationships without Bryan’s choice.

## Quick roleplay examples

These are optional lines and emotes, not past events. Each is under 255 characters.

Greeting: Hail. I’m Aelindra. Is there room for one more by the fire?

Meeting someone: I’m still learning these roads. Have you traveled this one before?

At a campfire: /e settles near the fire, leaving a little room beside her for Sylthara. She watches the sparks rise and smiles.

With Sylthara: /e crouches beside Sylthara and offers a quiet word, watching where the cat’s attention has settled.

With Thalorien: /e keeps a gentle eye on Thalorien as the young bear investigates the gathering.

Learning a trail: /e studies a footprint, then glances up. “I think it went this way. Would you take a look?”

Gentle humor: The ground holds still, yet somehow I still lose the trail.

Entering a dungeon: Sylthara and I will watch the flanks. Tell me where you need us.

A serious moment: Stay close. Let me take a look before we move.

Missing home: /e looks toward the open sky for a moment, her fingers brushing a pearl earring.

Ending a journey: Thank you for the company. I learned something today—and that is a good road by my measure.

## Hooks for other players

Ask about her visible markings, her bow, or how she pronounces Ser'jaen'thal. Offer to show her a local track or ask what she notices from a high vantage point. Invite her and the companion currently with her to a peaceful gathering. Leave her response and other characters’ choices open.
