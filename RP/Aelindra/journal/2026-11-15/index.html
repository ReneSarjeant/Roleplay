What the Fen Gave Back

Sylthara is asleep under the table at the Lion’s Pride, with one paw across my boot. I’ve moved twice for the ink. Both times she opened one eye until I put my foot back. It seems I am staying where I am until morning.

We’d already put a fair bit of road behind us before we reached the excavation. There was Night Watch business in Duskwood and unfinished business from the Stockade in Stormwind. By afternoon we were back in the Wetlands, where mist lies low enough to hide the little streams until you step in one. On Zephras, I could look over the edge and see what lay beneath me. Down here I’m still learning to watch what comes out of the grass. Sylthara generally knows before I do.

Archlight Halo was leading the party. When I was still finding my way around the outside of the dig, he sent me around from the south. Steverus Jagarius gave me the last bit of the trail: left of the meeting stone and up the hill. Sylthara and I were running by then. We fought through highland spiders on the approach, and I was glad to see friendly faces when we got there.

There were five of us going in. Tikito Malixi took the front, and Archlight watched over us with his healing. Chuds Maxley carried his totems, and Steverus was a hunter like me. He had Hawthorn with him, so Sylthara had company of her own. I took a moment to see who was where before we went into the swamp. It’s easier to call for someone when you’ve already put a face to the name.

The dig was open to the sky. Banks of dark earth rose between tall trunks, with tufts of rust-colored grass and loose boards underfoot. In places the trees were far enough apart for a clear shot. In others, a fold in the ground could hide a whole raptor. It was easy to look across the site and think you could see where you were going.

Saltspine was the first to correct that impression. A white crocolisk is easy enough to pick out against dark mud once you know where to look. The difficulty is remembering how much of it is tail when the head is all you can see. Sylthara went in close, and I found room to shoot. We brought it down quickly enough that I began to feel good about our chances.

Then we met Shadetooth.

The great violet raptor ruled a thicket full of smaller ones. Sylthara went down before the worst of it. Tikito fell, then Steverus, then Chuds. I remember how suddenly the space around me emptied. There was still time to draw, but precious little time to aim. Then I was down too.

We went back. I was glad to have Sylthara on her feet beside me, and I watched the ground between us as carefully as I watched the raptor. Shadetooth grew more furious as the fight wore on. By then we’d stayed on our feet long enough to make those last moments count. When the raptor finally fell, I looked for Sylthara before I looked at what we’d won.

There were people to look for in the dig as well. I found Daewyn Songblade and took on the duty of carrying word of his fate back to Dorin. Ardin Grassman had a reed-woven heart for Caitlin in Menethil. It weighed next to nothing. I tucked it away where the rest of my gear couldn’t crush it.

At the far end of the dig stood the Relic Guardian, a stone construct marked in green. Planks lay scattered around its clearing, and great roots and trunks cut across the view. It spoke of stabilization and defenses in the same even voice, as though we were a fault it meant to put right. Chuds’s totems stood in the dirt. My hawk went in from above while Sylthara worked close to the stone. I kept shooting until the Guardian lay on its side among the timbers, its green markings still visible from where I stood.

That was where I got the Golemsight Long Gun. I’d gone into the site with my bow. I came out with a gun worth buying good shot for and a Titan Relic that would take me all the way to Ironforge.

Coming out took some doing. We ran into Thicket Lurkers after the Guardian was down, and they put the whole party on the ground. Sylthara fell again. I’d already had the satisfaction of seeing the great stone thing topple, and there I was with wet earth against my cheek, undone by creatures that barely stood above the grass. I thought about that more than I thought about the gun on the way out.

In Lakeshire I gave Dorin the news about Daewyn. His was the hardest errand to finish. At Menethil Harbor I put the little woven heart into Caitlin’s hands, still whole after all that. I took the relic to Prospector Whelgar and made my last Wetlands stop with Rethiel the Greenwarden. Whelgar sent the relic on with me. High Explorer Magellas is to see it in the Hall of Explorers.

So tonight the gun is within reach, the relic is packed, and my cat has pinned me to a chair in Goldshire. I can hear the inn through the floorboards. Ironforge can have the relic tomorrow. Sylthara can have my boot a little longer.
