Backstory

Bram Copperkeep learned early that a full storeroom meant little if nobody knew what was in it. Working among Ironforge’s merchants, he kept ledgers, counted deliveries, and learned to question anyone who described a missing payment as a misunderstanding.

He trained with axe and shield to guard shipments, though he preferred cargo that arrived without anything needing to be hit. Eventually, he found steadier work handling supplies, sales, and accounts for adventurers—people remarkably good at finding treasure and remarkably careless about where they left it.

Bram lives in Ironforge, where he handles banking and auction-house business for his friends. He deposits their coin, keeps their supplies in order, and sells what they send home, making sure the proceeds reach the right purse.

Bram takes pride in paying fairly, packing carefully, and keeping something aside for lean days. He grumbles over muddy parcels and bags full of questionable salvage, but makes certain his friends leave supplied for the road.

His usual parting advice is simple: “Come back alive. We can settle the account then.”
