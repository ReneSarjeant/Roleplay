Brinn’s Shadow & Holy Priest Leveling Guide

Levels 10–60 · Shadow primary · Holy second specialization at 40
The plan: Respec now into Shadow for solo leveling. At 40 purchase dual specialization and allocate Holy independently. No planned reset at 30 or 40. These are leveling builds, not final raid builds. D/H/S denotes Discipline/Holy/Shadow; assumes one standard talent point per level from 10, without bonus Legacy points.

5. Playing the two specializations

Shadow: Use Shadow Word: Pain on enemies that will live long enough, Mind Blast as appropriate, Mind Flay once learned, and wand to finish enemies cheaply. Use Spirit Tap recovery between fights. Vampiric Embrace helps sustain you; Shadowform prevents healing spells, so leave it before direct healing. Use Silence on dangerous casts.

Holy: Train current healing ranks. Use Heal or Greater Heal for efficient tank healing, Renew for useful ongoing damage, Flash Heal for emergencies, Prayer of Mending for expected damage and Prayer of Healing for party damage. Holy Binding passively heals Brinn when Flash Heal targets another player. Alternate useful heals for Litany of Light, without wasting mana to force a refund. Carry water and communicate mana before pulls. Before 40, baseline healing spells remain available in Shadow outside Shadowform, but dedicated healing is not the priority.
