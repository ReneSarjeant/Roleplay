WoW Forever Priest Leveling Guide

Sister Brinn Sarjeant · Through Shadowform at 42 · Shadow + Holy at 40

Plan

Mostly solo open-world questing, with occasional dungeons for healing practice. Keep the early wand-and-Spirit Tap approach, continue into Shadow, and unlock Holy as Brinn’s second specialization at level 40. Bryan played Holy in Classic and has chosen it for Brinn’s dedicated healing role.

Recommended default: keep Wand Specialization and take Shadowform at 42. Dual specialization at 40 does not itself grant Shadowform.

Prerequisites

Get a usable wand by level 10 if possible: craft, buy, or earn one from a quest. Compare wand damage per second; spell damage does not add damage to wand attacks. Carry water, keep Fortitude and Inner Fire active, and train useful spell ranks.

Solo Questing

Open from range with Shadow Word: Pain and/or Mind Blast as appropriate. Use Devouring Plague on targets that will survive its damage, or when its healing is useful; do not automatically spend mana on every available spell.

Use Mind Flay for damage and slowing when helpful, then finish with your wand to conserve mana. Let mana regeneration work rather than repeatedly casting into an almost-dead target. Spirit Tap does not require a wand killing blow.

Use Power Word: Shield when incoming damage or spell pushback warrants it; precasting it is an option for a dangerous pull, not a requirement for every enemy. Heal between pulls as needed.

Use Psychic Scream carefully: fleeing enemies can bring more adds. Drink when mana is low; do not wait for an emergency.

Spell and Talent Changes

Improved Mind Flay now increases successive tick damage rather than providing the old damage-and-range package. Dark Inspiration adds a temporary party Spirit buff through Mind Blast or a completed Mind Flay channel.

Fear Ward and Devouring Plague are baseline at level 20; Divine Spirit at 30; Shadow Word: Death at 32. Devouring Plague’s cooldown is 24 seconds in the published updates.

Shadow Word: Death is an execute tool: if it hits without killing the target, the backlash is now 30% of your maximum health. Avoid using it casually at low health; misses do not cause backlash.

Holy Binding replaces Binding Heal in Holy. Flash Heal on another target also heals the priest for 50% of that healing, generating no threat from the self-heal.

Healing Before Dual Spec

A Shadow-leaning priest can heal occasional leveling dungeons with trained baseline healing spells, suitable gear, water, and a manageable group pace. Performance depends on the dungeon and party; talents alone do not guarantee success.

Use Heal or the appropriate trained direct heal for routine damage; Flash Heal for urgent damage. Use Renew where its ticks will be useful and shields for damage prevention or emergencies. Avoid blanket casting that wastes mana.

Dispel Magic and remove diseases as needed. Vampiric Embrace and damage spells can supplement healing when the party is stable, but direct healing comes first. Keep mana in reserve and communicate when a drink is needed.

Level 40: Shadow + Holy

Unlock dual specialization at 40 and use Holy for dedicated dungeon healing. Holy is Brinn’s chosen second specialization, matching Bryan’s Classic experience; Discipline is not the default plan.

Keep the leveling specialization at 2 Discipline / 0 Holy / 29 Shadow at level 40, then 2/0/30 at 41 and 2/0/31 with Shadowform at 42. At level 30 the allocation is exactly 2/0/19; at 39 it is 2/0/28.

Optional alternative: respec the leveling build to 0/0/31 at 40 for immediate Shadowform, moving the two wand points into Shadow. This costs a talent reset and sacrifices the wand bonus; the recommended default is to wait until 42.

Holy’s 31-point endpoint is Prayer of Mending and requires Spirit of Redemption. Holy Binding and Litany of Light are relevant healing options on the way. This guide establishes the second-spec role; it does not claim a tested point-by-point Holy build.

Shadowform reduces Shadow spell mana costs by 50%. Switch to the Holy specialization for dedicated healing rather than trying to heal through Shadowform. Visit a major-city class trainer for talent resets.

Approach and Verification

This is a recommended leveling path, not an in-game benchmark or universal best build. It assumes one normal talent point per level from 10, with no additional Legacy talent points. The 33 points allocated by level 42 are 2 Discipline and 31 Shadow; row thresholds are respected.

Keep Brinn’s gradual Shadow story separate from mechanical optimization: using Shadow does not erase her devotion to the Light. Exploration and steady progress remain the goal.
