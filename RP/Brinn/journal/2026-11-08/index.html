Sister Brinn Sarjeant · 8 November · Year 25 ADP

A Fine Day for Fishing

Brinn took a sealed crate out to Redridge in the morning. At Three Corners she turned for Elwynn, where there was better picking along the road. Peacebloom, silverleaf, earthroot. She named each one under her breath before she cut it. Maud had been gone two years, and still Brinn could hear exactly how she’d say, That one first, then.

By the time Brinn reached Stormwind, Mid Life Aggro had all but filled the Cathedral steps. The gnomes went in front, then the dwarves, then the humans. The hunters and their animals were to take the sides, if the animals could be persuaded. Brinn looked at all the faces turned toward the square.

“Everyone, let’s hug.”

It was perhaps the least practical suggestion anybody made that morning, right up until Naya suggested they go after the Sewer Beast before they went fishing.

Down they went to the canal. Toric called out, “Say your prayers, Sewer Beast!” Brinn got her wand up. Then the crocolisk was still, and all the people who’d had such trouble keeping quiet on the Cathedral steps had a good reason to make noise.

“We win,” Brinn said, and grinned.

They put their lines in between the Trade District and Old Town. Brinn caught mud snappers and a bristle whisker catfish. Naya pulled up a baby crocolisk, which seemed a very roundabout way to come home with another one. Brinn cheered loud enough for the other bank to hear. She usually liked her fishing quiet. She could make an exception.

There was time to learn the recipe for a Mana Well before she went back to the picking. By the time she came up the road to Sentinel Hill, she’d found mageroyal and felt ready to gather the harder herbs in Westfall. She had a bed at the inn. She’d have liked her own room in Goldshire, but this one would do. The People’s Militia could have her in the morning.
