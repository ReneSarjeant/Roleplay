# Dariand Nightwing

Night elf rogue. Old by the standards of those who take up the knife late. Skin the color of old bark. White hair pulled into a long tail. Face weathered past the point of looking surprised by anything. He stabs backs for a living now. He did not always.

## The Long Quiet

Dariand was born during the Long Vigil, centuries before the Third War. Exact years are not a thing he keeps. The settlement he served was small, set deep enough in the Ashenvale borders that the Sentinels only came through on a schedule. He was a tracker and boundary-walker. He read trails, set snares, kept the edges of the wood free of the things that did not belong, and reported what the Sentinels did not have time to see themselves. The work was quiet. It paid in meat, in quiet nights, and in the knowledge that the people behind him were still there when he came back.

He was already old when Nordrassil burned. The loss of immortality did not make him suddenly frail. It made the future finite in a way it had never been. The settlement felt it too. Some of the younger ones spoke of going to the new tree Fandral was growing off the coast. Dariand stayed. The trails were the same. The work was the same. He told himself the wood did not care about the change in the sky.

## The Settlement

The settlement is gone. He does not say how. When asked he says the wood changed and the people did not, and that is the end of the sentence. The tracks he used to follow are older than the trees that grew over them. He kept the skills. He stopped using them for the same reasons.

What he will not say is that the new mortality made some of the old protections feel thin. A boundary that had held for centuries failed in a single season. He came back from a long circuit and the place was already quiet. No bodies left for the scavengers. No clear tracks that led anywhere useful. The people he had walked the edges for were simply not there. He sat on the remains of a marker stone until the light went. Then he walked out.

## The Turn

He is old for an elf who decides, late, that the clean work is finished. Most who take the rogue’s path start younger, when the hands are still quick and the conscience is still flexible. Dariand’s hands are steady enough. The conscience was never the problem. What changed was the understanding that some doors do not open from the front, and some people do not stop because they were asked. The new mortality made that math clearer. A life that ends is a life that can be spent. He chose to spend what was left on work that did not require him to watch the edges for people who were already gone.

He learned the knife the way he once learned a trail: by walking it until the shape of it lived in the feet. He is not elegant. He is not fast the way the younger ones are fast. He is patient, and he does not miss when the opening is there. The back is simply the side that does not see the strike coming. He has made his peace with that math.

## Present

Dariand Nightwing works where the contract is clear and the target is already decided by someone else. He does not choose the names. He does not ask why the name was chosen. He takes the coin, does the work, and leaves before the questions start. The white tail and the weathered face make him easy to remember and hard to place. Most who meet him assume he is older than he looks and quieter than he is.

Darnassus is new. The tree is new. The city is still finding its shape. He does not live there. He walks the edges when the work is finished, the way he always did. The difference is that the edges now belong to whoever is paying for the silence behind them. The settlement is gone. The skills are not. The years ahead are no longer endless. He treats them accordingly.
