WoW Forever Priest Leveling Guide (to Level 39)

For Sister René Sarjeant · Mostly solo open-world questing · Dual-spec Shadow + Discipline at 40

Plan: Mostly solo open-world questing, with 1–2 dungeons for healing practice. Dual-spec into Shadow + Discipline at level 40.

This path prioritizes solo efficiency via Spirit Tap + Wand Specialization while still allowing solid healing performance in the occasional dungeon.

Prerequisites

Get a usable wand by level 10 if possible (Lesser Magic Wand via Enchanting, buy, or quest). Wand Specialization is extremely strong early.

By level 30 you should be around 2 Discipline / 0 Holy / 19 Shadow (or very close).

Levels 31–39

Continue investing in Shadow. Finish useful talents such as:

  • Remaining Shadow Focus
  • Improved Mind Blast
  • Blackout (stun chance for control)
  • Other available row talents

You do not need to respec into pure Discipline just for 1–2 dungeons. Meditation is strong (50% regen while casting) but not required for light dungeon use. Pure Disc for Penance is better if dungeon-spamming heavily.

Solo Questing Rotation

  1. Pre-cast Power Word: Shield.
  2. Apply Shadow Word: Pain (and Devouring Plague once available at 20 — now available to every race).
  3. Mind Blast on cooldown.
  4. Mind Flay when in range (once learned).
  5. Finish with the wand so you get the killing blow and Spirit Tap.
  6. Use Psychic Scream for extra adds.
  7. Sit and drink mainly when Spirit Tap is down and mana is low.
Notes:
• Divine Spirit becomes baseline at level 30.
• Fear Ward is baseline at level 20 for everyone.
• Keep upgrading your wand as you level — it remains a big part of your damage until Shadowform.

Healing the Occasional Dungeon

You can heal adequately as a Shadow-leaning priest:

  • Shield the tank aggressively.
  • Keep Renew up on anyone taking damage.
  • Use Flash Heal / Heal / Greater Heal reactively.
  • Once you have Vampiric Embrace, apply it and weave in SW:P / Mind Flay when the group is stable (passive party healing helps).
  • Dispel Magic as needed.
  • Tell the group you’re practicing so expectations stay reasonable.

This gives real multi-tasking practice before dual-spec.

At Level 40

Dual-spec into:

  • Full Shadow (Shadowform is the big power spike — better damage and mana efficiency) for continued solo/questing.
  • Discipline (Penance, Soul Warding, Meditation, Improved Power Word: Shield, etc.) for when you want to heal more seriously.

Respecs are done at class trainers. Dual spec itself unlocks at 40.

Philosophy Fit

This keeps you efficient in the open world (your main activity), gives practical healing experience on those dungeon runs, and sets up a clean transition at 40. Progress over perfection.
