1–60 Beast Mastery Hunter + Sylthara Leveling Guide

For Aelindra Sarjeant · WoW Forever · Secondary specialization: Marksmanship at level 40

Sylthara is the Prideclaw cat from Zephras Isle (Ferocity family). Cats deal high damage with thin armor and use Prowl.

Solo Playstyle

Send Sylthara in first and let it build threat for a couple of seconds. Apply Hunter’s Mark on anything that will take time. Use Serpent Sting on longer fights. Stay at range with Auto Shot under Aspect of the Hawk. Once available, use Summon Hawk on cooldown for longer fights (it shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot — favor the hawk when the fight will last). Use Aimed Shot on single targets and Multi-Shot on packs. Arcane Shot fills gaps when mana allows. Intimidation controls tough targets or recovers threat. Bestial Wrath (once unlocked) is the pet burst cooldown. Mend Pet early. Feign Death and Concussive Shot for safety or resets.

Keep your bow and ammo upgraded. Dual Specialization opens at level 40 — set the secondary to Marksmanship.

Sylthara Pet Leveling

Pets gain roughly 25% of your experience and only while out and contributing. Keep Sylthara active.

Happiness is the biggest multiplier:
• Happy (green) = 125% damage
• Content (yellow) = 100%
• Unhappy (red) = 75%

Feed meat or fish regularly. Carry stacks and feed before long sessions or after hard fights.

Loyalty raises the training points available (roughly Pet Level × (Loyalty Rank − 1)). Consistent feeding and successful combat build it. Avoid repeated deaths or neglect.

Core abilities for a cat: Growl (threat), Bite and Claw (damage), Dash (mobility), Prowl (family ability — stealth with a damage bonus on the first attack out of stealth). Teach higher ranks as soon as pet level and training points allow.

Cats are squishy. Mend early and watch focus. Use Prowl for openers or quiet positioning. Stable when needed.

End-Game Ability Hunt

Once Sylthara is high level (especially 50+), unlock the top ranks so the Prideclaw hits as hard as possible. Cats can learn Bite, Claw, Dash, and Prowl.

Priority targets:

  • Bite Rank 8 and Claw Rank 8 (pet level 56): High-level Winterspring beasts such as Elder Shardtooth and Winterspring Screecher.
  • Prowl Rank 3 (pet level 50): Jaguero Stalker in Stranglethorn Vale (also available on some Frostsaber Stalkers in Winterspring).
  • Dash Rank 3 (pet level 50): Various high-level cats and other Dash-capable beasts (Frostsabers, certain panthers, etc.). Bloodaxe Worg in Lower Blackrock Spire is a classic high-rank source if accessible.

How to teach:

  1. Tame a temporary beast that knows the desired high-rank ability (Beast Lore confirms it when available).
  2. The rank becomes available in your training pool.
  3. Abandon or stable the temporary pet.
  4. With Sylthara summoned, Aelindra can use her Train Pet spell to teach Sylthara the rank, if the cat family can learn it and Sylthara meets the required level and has enough training points.

You only need to unlock each rank once. After that, any eligible pet can learn it. Do this after Sylthara has enough training points.

Broken Tooth (Badlands rare, 1.0 attack speed) is a useful temporary fast-attack cat if you want the speed, but keep Sylthara as the main companion.
