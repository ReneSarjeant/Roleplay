Warcraft Lore from Year -10,000 to Year 25

After the Great Sundering — the Long Vigil, the high elves, the Third War, and the present

A brief continuation of the previous page, focused on the elven histories relevant to these characters. This is a selective overview, not a complete chronology. Numbered references distinguish classic Warcraft history, published Forever lore, and Bryan’s character canon.

Dates and scope: This repository uses Year 25 after the first opening of the Dark Portal as its roleplay present, and approximately Year -10,000 for the Sundering. These are the page’s chronological frame. Blizzard describes Forever as occupying the original WoW era in its own time bubble; its panel recap calls this an early “Year 1” period, rather than explicitly specifying Year 25 after the Dark Portal. The two labels should not be treated as the same calendar statement.[5]

The Long Vigil and Nordrassil

After the Sundering, night elf survivors rebuilt around Mount Hyjal. Alexstrasza grew Nordrassil from an enchanted acorn in the new Well of Eternity. Nozdormu’s enchantment protected the night elves from aging and disease; Ysera linked the tree to the Emerald Dream, with the druids agreeing to long periods of sleep. Malfurion and the three Aspects made a pact to safeguard the Well against the Legion’s return. This was not simply a blessing binding the Aspects to guard the tree.[1]

The kaldorei turned away from arcane spellcasting toward druidism. During the Long Vigil, Tyrande’s Sentinels guarded their homeland while the druids slept.[1]

The high elves and Quel’Thalas

Dath’Remar’s Highborne followers defied the prohibition on arcane magic by unleashing a storm over Ashenvale. They were exiled, sailed east, and became known as high elves.[1] Led by Dath’Remar Sunstrider, the exiles eventually founded Quel’Thalas. They created the Sunwell using water preserved from the original Well of Eternity, establishing a new source of arcane power.[2]

During the Third War, Arthas’s Scourge invaded Quel’Thalas and defeated Silvermoon’s defenders. Arthas used the Sunwell to resurrect Kel’Thuzad, polluting its waters.[3]

The Skyborne (shen’dorei) — Forever lore

Blizzard’s Forever account identifies the Skyborne’s founders as rebels who fled Eldre’Thalas after the War of the Ancients and eventually found refuge in Skywall. Wind spirits anchored their island home there. The published overview does not give an exact year for that migration, so it is not dated more precisely here.[5]

On Zephras Isle, the disappearance of those benefactors has left protective structures failing and communities isolated. The Windshapers seek their missing mentors and the preservation of their elemental traditions; the High Order seeks lost arcane knowledge to make the shen’dorei less dependent on those patrons. They align with the Horde and Alliance respectively.[6] The Al’Aketh is another group competing for control; the two playable orders are not the island’s only factions.[5]

Shen’dorei Lore — Children of the Hidden Sky provides the longer account, including separately identified beta material and Aelindra’s personal history. The summary above relies directly on Blizzard’s published Forever articles.

The Third War and the loss of immortality

The Legion and its Scourge allies brought war to Kalimdor. Guided by Medivh, the forces led by Thrall, Jaina, Tyrande, and Malfurion united at Mount Hyjal. Malfurion unleashed Nordrassil’s power against Archimonde, defeating him.[3] The victory left Nordrassil burned and the night elves without their immortality.[4]

Teldrassil and Darnassus

After the war, Fandral Staghelm and fellow druids planted Teldrassil off northern Kalimdor in an attempt to recover immortality. Darnassus grew within its branches, but the tree did not receive the blessing they sought, and corruption spread through its wildlife and limbs. This describes the original WoW setting; later retail developments are outside this page’s scope.[3]

Character canon: Dariand Nightwing’s move to Darnassus, work on the forest’s boundaries, and departure when that work ceased to feel protective belong to Bryan’s original character biography. They are not events established by Blizzard’s historical account.[7]

Year 25 — the roleplay present

These sheets place their characters in a world shaped by the Third War: Quel’Thalas has suffered the Scourge invasion, and the night elves have lost their immortality.[3] Forever adds the Skyborne’s crisis and return to Azeroth within its original-era setting.[5] “Year 25” remains the repository’s roleplay date; the cited Forever overview establishes the era, not that exact numeric date.

Sources and footnotes

Official PDF. Supports the Third War summary and original-era Teldrassil account. This overview does not repeat the manual’s sweeping claim that no living elves remained in Quel’Thalas or its older kingdom-age estimate.
  4. Blizzard Entertainment, World of Warcraft Classic, “Crown of the Earth,” quest 929, Corithras Moonrage’s completion dialogue. Game-text transcription on Wowhead.   6. Blizzard Entertainment, “WoW: Forever Meet the New Skyborne.” Official article, “Begin Your Journey on Zephras Isle,” “Choose Horde or Alliance at Character Creation,” and the Windshaper and High Order descriptions. Supports the present crisis, both orders’ goals, and faction alignments.
  7. Bryan’s original character canon, “Dariand Nightwing.” Repository biography at the reviewed revision, sections “The Long Vigil” and “Darnassus.” This is a source for Dariand’s personal history only, not independent evidence for Warcraft history.
