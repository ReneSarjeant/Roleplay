WoW Forever Priest Macros
========================

Console setting:
/console groundEffectDensity 256

---

Dispel Magic (mouseover with modifiers):
/showtooltip Dispel Magic
/stopcasting
/cast [@mouseover,nomod,exists] Dispel Magic;  [@mouseover,exists,mod:ctrl] Cure Disease; [@mouseover,exists,mod:shift] Dispel Magic

---

Power Word: Fortitude (with wave emote and shoot fallback):
#showtooltip Power Word: Fortitude
/cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][] Power Word: Fortitude
/wave [@mouseover,help,nodead][help,nodead]
/cast shoot [@mouseover,harm,nodead][harm,nodead]

---

Shoot:
#Showtooltip Shoot
/cast Shoot
