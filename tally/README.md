# Tally Copperkeep

- **Race:** Ironforge dwarf
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Residence:** Ironforge
- **Work:** Banking, auctions, and supplies for her friends

## Backstory

Tally Copperkeep learned early that a full storeroom meant little if nobody knew what was in it. Working among Ironforge’s merchants, she kept ledgers, counted deliveries, and learned to question anyone who described a missing payment as a misunderstanding.

She trained with axe and shield to guard shipments, though she preferred cargo that arrived without anything needing to be hit. Eventually, she found steadier work handling supplies, sales, and accounts for adventurers—people remarkably good at finding treasure and remarkably careless about where they left it.

Tally lives in Ironforge, where she handles banking and auction-house business for her friends. She deposits their coin, keeps their supplies in order, and sells what they send home, making sure the proceeds reach the right purse.

Tally takes pride in paying fairly, packing carefully, and keeping something aside for lean days. She grumbles over muddy parcels and bags full of questionable salvage, but makes certain her friends leave supplied for the road.

Her usual parting advice is simple: “Come back alive. We can settle the account then.”
