Warcraft Lore from Year -10,000 to Year 25

After the Great Sundering — the Long Vigil, the high elves, the Third War, and the present

A brief continuation of the previous page. Focuses on the major turns that shaped the world the characters now walk. Skyborne history is summarized only enough to place them, then linked to their full sourced page.

The Long Vigil and Nordrassil

After the Sundering the surviving night elves withdrew to the slopes of Mount Hyjal. The World Tree Nordrassil was planted above a new Well of Eternity. Its blessing granted the night elves immortality and bound the dragon Aspects to protect the tree. The Long Vigil began: the night elves stood watch against any return of the Burning Legion. Arcane magic was largely set aside by the remaining kaldorei.

The high elves and Quel’Thalas

Some Highborne who had survived the war continued to practice arcane magic. After a magical storm they unleashed, they were exiled from Kalimdor. Led by Dath’Remar Sunstrider they crossed the sea, founded Quel’Thalas, and used a vial of the original Well to create the Sunwell. Over time they became the high elves. Their kingdom stood for thousands of years until the Third War.

The Skyborne (shen’dorei)

A separate group of Highborne from Eldre’Thalas (later Dire Maul) also refused the path taken by others of their caste. After the War of the Ancients they fled and found refuge with wind spirits in the Elemental Plane of Air. There they established Zephras Isle and other floating settlements. Over millennia they became the shen’dorei, or Skyborne — a people shaped by the sky and by their long isolation. In the present of Year 25 their island is failing, and two major orders (the Windshapers and the High Order) have begun to seek aid from the wider world.

Their full history, the sources that support it, and the distinctions between published lore, beta material, and character canon are kept on their own page.

The Third War and the loss of immortality

In the years leading to Year 25 the Burning Legion returned. The Scourge and the orcish Horde crossed into Kalimdor. At the Battle of Mount Hyjal the night elves, humans, and orcs together stopped Archimonde, but Nordrassil was badly damaged. The night elves’ immortality ended. The new tree Teldrassil was later grown off the northern coast; the city of Darnassus rose among its branches. Corruption entered the new forest. Some who had walked the old edges, such as Dariand Nightwing, left when the work no longer felt like protection.

Year 25

The present setting of these sheets is Year 25 after the Dark Portal. The Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor are still recovering from the Third War. The high elves have suffered the fall of Silvermoon. The night elves are mortal again. The Skyborne are only now re-entering the wider world. The characters on these pages walk that ground — some as survivors of older eras, some as people shaped by the more recent wars, and one as a young hunter who has known nothing but the sky until now.
